A change to Reformer

Mat classes stay exactly the same. Here's how to keep Reformer in your week.

Legacy offers close Fri 13 Nov

First, thank you. 🙏

Since we opened the doors of our Robina studio, you've been with us, helping to build such a warm, strong community in our Reformer room. Because this community matters so much to us, we want to take a moment to be honest with you about where things are at.

As you can imagine, running small-group Reformer is a team effort. It requires skilled teachers, effective studio space, quality equipment and an intimate class size, so everyone gets real attention. It's something we're really proud of.

Over the past 3 years we've been so grateful to work with Fitness Passport (FP) to welcome all of you into our studio, and we value each and every one of you. You may be aware that FP pays us a set amount (approximately $8.00) for each visit, and for Reformer classes that amount doesn't cover our costs, particularly our teachers' wages.

To ease this, in 2025 we stopped offering Reformer to new Fitness Passport members, while protecting your access as a long-term member with your 4x monthly credits. Since then we've continued to look at every way possible to keep things as they are, but we've reached the point where we can't continue offering Reformer at no extra cost.

Reformer access is rare at Fitness Passport studios, and we're proud to have offered it, but sadly we are unable to sustain it as it stands. We've deliberated for more than a year, looking at many options, and at one point wondering if we should remove FP from Robina altogether. That's not what we want. Instead, we've come up with another way that we hope will be equitable for everyone.

From Friday 13 November, your 4 monthly Reformer credits will end. Your mat classes stay exactly as they are, and you'll still scan in as usual for each class. However, any Fitness Passport member will be able to keep Reformer in their week with an add-on 10-Pack or weekly membership.

As a legacy member, you also have two extra options.

We know how much you've valued your monthly credits, so you're invited to the exclusive legacy offers below, which won't be offered to anyone else.
